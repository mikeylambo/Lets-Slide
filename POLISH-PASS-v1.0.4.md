2. Landing quality feedback: clean/hard landing flashes plus existing impact camera response.
3. Route readability: fast/mastery route beacons; score line remains pickup-readable.
4. Ghost presentation: author/PB treatments separated, ghost opacity setting, checkpoint PB splits.
5. PB delta: checkpoint-relative delta derived from the recorded PB ghost.

8. Accessibility/presentation settings: camera shake, speed FX, Flow/INVERT FX, ghost opacity, FOV scale.
9. Menu identity: animated contour-map field with a quiet 174 BPM grid.
10. Attract loop: idle author-line preview after 18 seconds without mutating records.
